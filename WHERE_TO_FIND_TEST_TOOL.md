## 🔍 Troubleshooting

### "I don't see the orange button on Dashboard"

**Check:**
1. Are you on the Dashboard? (Main screen after login)
2. Look for "Quick Actions" heading
3. It's the 4th tile (bottom right)
4. Color: Orange, Icon: Science flask 🧪

**If still not visible:**
- Try hot reload: Press `r` in terminal
- Or restart app: Press `R` in terminal

### "I don't see it on Progress screen"

**It's at the very bottom!**
1. Open Progress screen
2. Scroll ALL the way down
3. Past the milestones
4. Past "Return to Dashboard" button
5. You'll see two buttons side by side

### "Button is there but it's gray, not orange"

- On Progress screen, left button is orange ("Test Achievements")
- Right button is gray ("Reset All")
- On Dashboard, the tile should be orange
